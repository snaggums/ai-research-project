# VS-04.2 — Workflow Clarity and Research Progress

## Vertical Slice

- **Name:** Workflow Clarity and Research Progress
- **Version:** 1.1
- **Status:** Planned and deferred until after the finished MVP
- **Target duration:** 8–10 working days, excluding approval wait time
- **Owner:** Sky AIR
- **Depends on:** Approved Session, Transcript, Session Report, Record, Record
  Synthesis, Record Knowledge, Figma design-system, Storybook, and responsive
  contracts

## Product Goal

Make workflow completion understandable from every Session and Record workspace
so researchers can tell what is complete, what is blocking the next stage, and
what single action they should take next.

This slice reports workflow completion. It does not attempt to score research
quality.

## Approved Terminology

- **Workflow status** communicates completion and the next recommended action.
- **Record synthesis eligibility** communicates whether a Session can
  contribute to its related Record synthesis.
- **Record readiness** communicates whether a Record can generate, review,
  approve, or regenerate synthesis.
- **Processing Status** remains the operational state for asynchronous work and
  is not replaced by workflow status.

## Current Behavior

- Session processing, Session Report status, Record readiness, synthesis scope,
  and synthesis actions appear in separate parts of the interface.
- The Record synthesis scope identifies included and excluded Sessions, but the
  complete eligibility rule is not stated consistently at both the Session and
  Record levels.
- Several pages repeat full Page Header and summary compositions, consuming
  vertical space.
- Session and Record pages may expose more than one plausible next action.
- Session Report, Theme, and Record synthesis regeneration consequences are not
  expressed through one consistent confirmation contract.
- The backend exposes the canonical source data needed to derive workflow state
  but does not expose a structured workflow response with stable reason and
  action codes.

## Approved Domain Rules

### Research progress

- Research progress measures workflow completion only.
- Participants do not count toward progress and never block eligibility.
- Progress and synthesis eligibility are related but distinct:
  - a Researcher Reviewed or Approved Session Report may be eligible;
  - a fully complete Session workflow requires an Approved Session Report;
  - a Record may have reviewed synthesis items before its workflow is complete;
    and
  - a complete Record workflow requires all applicable synthesis items to be
    Approved.

### Session workflow steps

1. Session details provided.
2. Record assigned.
3. Primary Transcript processed.
4. Session Report generated.
5. Session Report reviewed.
6. At least one evidence-linked Requirement, Decision, or Action Item
   available.
7. Session Report approved.

Participants may be displayed as optional Session information but are excluded
from the progress denominator and blocking logic.

### Record synthesis eligibility

A related Session is eligible when its latest Session Report:

1. has status Researcher Reviewed or Approved; and
2. contains at least one evidence-linked Requirement, Decision, or Action Item.

The existing minimum of two eligible related Sessions remains unchanged.
Unresolved Decision maker or Assignee ownership does not block eligibility; it
blocks final approval only.

### Record workflow steps

1. Related Sessions available.
2. At least two related Sessions eligible.
3. Synthesis generated.
4. Synthesis reflects the latest eligible Session Report revisions.
5. Synthesis items reviewed.
6. Synthesis items approved.

### Session primary-action priority

Only the first applicable action is presented as primary:

1. **Upload transcript**
2. **Generate Session Report**
3. **Review report**
4. **Approve report**
5. **Open related Record**

### Record primary-action priority

Only the first applicable action is presented as primary:

1. **Open Sessions** when the Record has no related Sessions.
2. **Review Session Reports** when fewer than two related Sessions are eligible;
   open the first blocking Session Report.
3. **Generate synthesis** when at least two Sessions are eligible and no
   synthesis exists.
4. **Review synthesis** when AI-generated items remain; open the first
   unresolved item.
5. **Approve synthesis items** when Researcher Reviewed items remain; open the
   first item awaiting approval.
6. **Open Record knowledge** when all applicable items are approved and the
   synthesis is current.

Exception actions take precedence:

1. **Retry synthesis** after a failed run.
2. **Regenerate synthesis** when eligible source Report revisions have changed
   since the latest completed run.

## UX Objectives

- Show workflow completion without presenting a research-quality score.
- Explain the difference between processing, eligibility, and completion.
- Present one state-aware primary action per Session or Record page.
- Reduce repeated header height by composing existing approved components more
  compactly.
- Make Session-to-Record eligibility and Record-to-Session blockers navigable.
- Confirm regeneration before reviewed work is superseded.
- Preserve approved Figma and responsive behavior.

## User Stories

- As a researcher, I want to know the next required Session action so that I can
  prepare the Session for Record synthesis.
- As a researcher, I want to know whether a Session contributes to its related
  Record so that I can resolve an eligibility blocker at its source.
- As a researcher, I want a Record to identify the first blocking Session Report
  so that I can make synthesis possible without interpreting several status
  panels.
- As a researcher, I want to know whether a synthesis is missing, failed,
  outdated, awaiting review, or awaiting approval so that I take the correct
  next action.
- As a researcher, I want regeneration consequences explained before proceeding
  so that reviewed work is never silently replaced.

## Information Architecture

No new primary route or navigation destination is introduced.

Contextual navigation:

- **View Record readiness** from a Session opens the related Record overview and
  moves focus to the Record readiness section.
- **Open Session Report** from an included Session opens that Session Report.
- **Open Session Report** from an excluded Session opens that Session Report so
  the researcher can resolve its blocker.

Preferred anchored destination:

```text
/records/{recordId}#synthesis-readiness
```

The final route and focus behavior must be approved with the Figma contract.

- `Workflow Status`
  - Session in progress;
  - Session eligible but approval incomplete;
  - Session complete;

  - recoverable error;
  - primary action and no-action states.
- `Record Synthesis Eligibility`
  - eligible;

  - no related Sessions;
  - insufficient eligible Sessions;

  - Themes;
  - unapproved Session Report;

### Existing component relationships

- Keep `Processing Status` separate for queued, processing, complete, and error
  operations.
- Keep `Record Summary` as stable Record metadata and remove duplicated
  readiness messaging once `Record Readiness` owns it.
- Keep `Record Synthesis Scope Summary` as the detailed included/excluded
  Session explanation.
- Consolidate the page-local Record `Synthesis action` panel into
  `Record Readiness`.
- Do not create a new `CompactWorkspaceHeader` component. Compose the approved
  Page Header, Session Summary Strip, and Record Summary components.

### Page-template updates

- Every Session tab uses the approved compact header composition.
- Every Record tab uses the approved compact header composition.
- Session Overview presents workflow status and Record synthesis eligibility.
- Session Report presents the current primary report action.
- Record Overview presents Record readiness and the synthesis scope.
- Record Knowledge presents the current review or approval action without
  duplicating Record Overview messaging.

### Regeneration dialog copy

#### Record synthesis

**Regenerate synthesis?**

AIR will create a new synthesis using the latest eligible Session Reports. When
generation succeeds, the new synthesis will replace the version currently shown
on this Record.

The previous synthesis will be retained for traceability, but previous versions
cannot currently be viewed or restored in AIR. The existing synthesis will
remain unchanged if generation fails.

Previously reviewed or approved items will not automatically transfer to the
new synthesis.

Actions:

- **Cancel**
- **Regenerate synthesis**

Approved Session Reports continue to use **Create revision**, not
**Regenerate**.

## Frontend API and MSW Deliverables

The exact endpoint paths require API-contract approval. Responses should use
semantic codes rather than presentation-ready labels or URLs:

```text
WorkflowResponse
  object_type
  object_id
  completed_steps
  total_steps
  steps[]
    code
    state
    blocking_reason_code?
  next_recommended_action
    code
    target_object_id?
  eligible_for_record?
```

## Backend Deliverables

- Derive Session workflow state from canonical Session, Record assignment,
  Primary Transcript, Session Report, Report item, and evidence data.
- Derive Record workflow state from related Sessions, eligibility, latest
  synthesis run, exact source Report revisions, and synthesis item statuses.
- Preserve the current eligibility rule:
  - latest Report status is Researcher Reviewed or Approved; and
  - at least one evidence-linked Requirement, Decision, or Action Item exists.
- Exclude Participants from progress and eligibility.
- Treat unresolved Decision maker or Assignee ownership as an approval blocker,
  not an eligibility blocker.
- Return structured steps and stable reason/action codes.
- Return semantic codes; do not return interface labels or constructed frontend
  URLs.
- Preserve exact synthesis source snapshots.
- Never silently regenerate or replace approved work.
- Keep derivation idempotent and free of workflow-state side effects.

## Database Changes

No workflow-state persistence or migration is planned.

Existing canonical objects, IDs, relationships, Report revisions, Synthesis
Runs, source snapshots, and provenance remain authoritative. Any later decision
to persist workflow snapshots requires a separate approval and replay-safe
migration.

- Do not communicate workflow state through color alone.
- Provide text labels for completed, current, and blocked steps.
- Keep exactly one page-level primary action in the workflow component.
- Move focus to the contextual destination heading after navigation.

- Announce asynchronous action status without moving focus unexpectedly.
- Preserve the approved AIR focus ring and logical heading order.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Research progress reports workflow completion and never claims to measure
   research quality.
2. Participants do not change progress or eligibility.
3. Session progress distinguishes Reviewed eligibility from Approved completion.
4. A Session explains whether it contributes to its related Record synthesis.
5. A Record identifies the first action required to become synthesis-ready.
6. Every Session and Record tab uses the approved compact composition.
7. Only one state-aware primary action is presented on each page.
8. Processing Status remains visible for asynchronous operational work.
9. Record Summary no longer duplicates readiness owned by Record Readiness.
10. Record Synthesis Scope Summary continues to list included and excluded
    Sessions with reasons.
11. Included and excluded Sessions link to their Session Reports.
12. Regeneration never silently overwrites reviewed or approved work.
13. Record regeneration copy states that the preserved prior run is not
    currently viewable or restorable.
14. Workflow state is derived and no workflow persistence migration is added.
15. Storybook, React, API/MSW, backend, tests, and accessibility checks pass.

## Risks and Dependencies

- Compact composition changes touch every Session and Record tab and can cause
  responsive regressions even though only Desktop source variants are authored.
- The exact destination of **Open Sessions** must preserve sufficient Project
  context for a workspace-level Record.
- A later synthesis-history interface must not reinterpret the MVP
  regeneration copy as a restoration promise.
- Stable action codes become a compatibility contract between backend and
  React.
- Existing route-specific actions must be audited so that consolidation does
  not hide required secondary actions.
